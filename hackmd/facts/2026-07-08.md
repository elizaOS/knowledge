# Fact Briefing: 2026-07-08

## Overall Summary
elizaOS is undergoing major architectural and structural shifts, including a migration away from the Ai16z platform and the dissolution of the Labs entity. Development remains active with significant focus on security hardening, monetization rails, and production-grade cloud infrastructure.

## Key Facts

- elizaOS team completed a migration away from the Ai16z platform to seek better traction elsewhere.
- The ElizaOS Labs entity was dissolved in April 2026, though the foundation remains active for open-source support.
- Elizacloud clarifed its security posture by utilizing SOC 2 Type II certified providers instead of holding direct certification.
- PR #11150 promoted 444 commits to production, implementing money-integrity measures and atomic crypto top-up rails.
- Developers implemented an 'intent routing' system to replace simple keyword matching for agent request processing.
- The project reached a milestone in agent voice interactions by integrating native support for OpenWakeWordDetector.
- Community members reported a significant decline in Eliza token price, attributed by users to conflicts with external figures.
- Strategic migration of the Eliza token to the Ethereum ecosystem has faced criticism for allegedly inflating token supply.
