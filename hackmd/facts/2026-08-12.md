# Fact Briefing: 2026-08-12

## Overall Summary
The ElizaOS project is experiencing a period of intense economic instability and community friction following disclosures regarding founder token liquidations and exchange delisting warnings. Simultaneously, technical development remains high-velocity with a successful transition of develop to main, emphasizing infrastructure hardening and the introduction of new voice capabilities.

## Key Facts

- PR #18324 promoting develop to main was merged on August 11, including aligned tests and image cache retries.
- Bybit exchange applied an ST (Special Treatment) tag to the ElizaOS token due to low liquidity issues.
- Founder Shaw Walters disclosed selling $25M worth of ElizaOS tokens to cover tax obligations, retaining $40K.
- The project standardized its runtime environment across all workflows to Bun 1.3.14.
- A successful transportation AI-agent implementation was reported using the ElizaOS framework by user 0x_cup.
- PR #17709 integrated Fish Audio streaming technology for real-time voice turns.
- PR #18175 migrated settings to per-connector detail pages with specific Delegate/Bot lenses.
- PR #17424 launched the eliza.army contribution skill and leaderboard system.
- The development team initiated a strategic move toward modularity by planning core component extraction into standalone repositories.
