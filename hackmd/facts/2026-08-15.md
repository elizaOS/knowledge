# Fact Briefing: 2026-08-15

## Overall Summary
ElizaOS is undergoing a major strategic pivot away from the web3 token economy toward a web2 consumer adoption model while simultaneously hardening infrastructure through the consolidation of Eliza Cloud into eliza.app. Despite structural leadership transitions and community criticism of legacy tokenomics, technical development continues with new visual workflow tools and high-volume bug remediation.
