#### Priority B — Eliminate fork-PR gate timeouts caused by `action_required` (High impact, Medium risk)
**Why now:** It burns CI minutes and produces misleading red checks, directly harming external contributor experience and maintainer throughput.

**Recommended approach**
- In aggregate gates, detect `action_required` and **fail fast with actionable status**:
  - “Waiting for maintainer approval to run fork workflows: [list]. Re-run gates after approval.”
- Alternatively: skip/pause aggregate gates until approval is granted, then re-evaluate.

**Dependencies**
- Requires agreement on policy for fork PR required checks and how to represent “not yet runnable.”

**Success metrics**
- Runner minutes wasted per fork PR reduced toward **0**
- “False red” required checks on first-time contributor PRs reduced toward **0**

---

#### Priority C — Manage mega-refactor risk (PR #17585: huge deletion volume) (High impact, High risk)
**Why now:** Extremely large diffs are where reliability regressions and review bottlenecks happen, especially amid ongoing modularization.

**Mitigations**
- Enforce PR slicing: require this change to land as a sequence of smaller PRs with clear invariants (CI, package graph integrity, release artifacts).
- Add a “refactor safety checklist” gate: build graph diff report, removed-path allowlist, and a dependency-map validation.

**Success metrics**
- Review cycle time for refactors reduced (median time-to-merge)
- Post-merge regressions attributable to refactor PRs reduced
