## 2) User Experience Intelligence

### Feedback categorized by impact & theme

**High impact (trust / retention)**

   - Users explicitly request an official statement on whether the token is abandoned.
   - Sentiment split: some accept product-first pivot; others view “token done” as reputational damage post-migration.
   - Impact: suppresses community advocacy, increases churn, and reframes all progress as “too late.”

2) **Scam exposure during support moments (safety risk)**

   - Impact: direct user harm risk + brand damage + increased mod burden.

**Medium impact (contributor conversion)**
3) **Onboarding/documentation discoverability**
   - Repeated asks for “start here docs” and how to contribute to eliza.army; answers lacked direct links.
   - Impact: reduces contributor activation and increases repetitive Q&A load.

**Low-to-medium impact (messaging / expectations)**
4) **“Working privately” narrative without artifacts**
   - Community hears “all-devices app soon,” but without a public checklist, beta plan, or timeline.
   - Impact: speculation cycles (abandonment rumors), weak expectation management.

### Usage patterns vs intended design
- Intended: shift attention to **shipping consumer product + contribution economy** (eliza.army).
- Observed: community still anchors success to **token price/utility** and treats silence as negative signal.
- Implication: product execution alone will not fix sentiment unless paired with explicit policy/positioning and a visible release plan.

### Implementation opportunities (UX + operations)
- Convert recurring Discord questions into **canonical pinned answers**:
  - “Token status and utility (current + future)”
  - “Where to start contributing (links + first issue flow)”
  - “Official support process (no DMs, no links, where to verify)”
- Add “support-safe” interaction design in Discord:
  - Auto-moderation for “support ticket” link patterns.
  - Bot message that triggers on keywords (“deposit missing”, “support”, “ticket”) with official guidance.

### Community sentiment (Aug 2–4 snapshot)
- Net sentiment: **anxious / skeptical**, with pockets of **defensive optimism** (price-drawdown context, “ship product and revenue” thesis).
- Risk: absent authoritative messaging, the skeptical narrative (“abandoned”) becomes the default interpretation, regardless of GitHub velocity.
