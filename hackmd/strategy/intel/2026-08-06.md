### 3) Strategic Prioritization (Impact vs Risk, Dependencies, Resource Allocation)

#### A) Immediate (0–7 days): Stabilize trust & reduce operational risk
1) **Publish an official status + scope boundary**
   - Deliverable: one signed statement clarifying:
     - What ElizaOS is (OSS scope, product scope)
     - What is explicitly out-of-scope (token promises, if any)
     - Communication cadence (weekly dev update minimum)
   - **User impact:** very high (reduces uncertainty-driven churn)
   - **Technical risk:** low
   - **Dependency:** leadership alignment + legal review (given lawsuit context)

2) **Discord safety hardening**
   - Actions:
     - Lock down “support” language: official support channel + bot auto-warning on “DM for support”
     - Add anti-scam automod rules; rapid delete for promos
     - Pinned “Never trust DMs” + official links list
   - **User impact:** high
   - **Risk:** low
   - **Resource:** 1 mod owner + 1 automation pass

3) **Restore developer onboarding**
   - Deliverable: “Start here” docs + contributor path (public, linkable)
   - Minimum: install/run instructions, where to ask questions, how to find “good first issues”
   - **User impact:** high (developer conversion)
   - **Risk:** low
   - **Dependency:** none

#### B) Near-term (2–4 weeks): Rebuild signal quality and execution efficiency
4) **Create “token / legal / historical” containment lane**
   - Put token discourse in a dedicated channel with strict moderation and a single pinned FAQ.
   - Keep core channels product/OSS-focused to recover engineering feedback loops.
   - **User impact:** medium-high (reduces channel noise, improves retention of builders)
   - **Risk:** medium (backlash if perceived as silencing; mitigate with transparency + archived logs)

5) **Adopt separation model as a concrete operating proposal (if pursued)**
   - The community-proposed split (OSS dev vs commercialization/token ops) should be evaluated as a formal option:
     - Define entity boundaries, IP licensing, revenue streams (cloud, enterprise support)
     - Governance: who decides roadmap, funding, trademarks
   - **User impact:** high (clear structure)
   - **Risk:** high (legal complexity; reputational risk if ambiguous)
   - **Critical dependencies:** legal counsel, IP/trademark ownership clarity, maintainers willing to operate OSS regardless of token status

6) **Engineer productivity: reduce large-diff blast radius**
   - Given prevalence of sweeping refactors and CI ordering issues, prioritize:
     - Smaller PR slicing requirements for non-emergency changes
     - CI determinism fixes (e.g., Turbo ordering edge issue like Calendar↔Video typecheck dependency)
     - Fork-PR gate behavior improvements (avoid timeouts when checks are `action_required`)
   - **User impact:** medium (contributors get faster merges; fewer red-herring failures)
   - **Risk:** medium (process friction; mitigate with “exception policy”)

#### C) Resource allocation recommendation (next sprint)
- **40%**: Trust & comms infrastructure (status page, cadence, onboarding docs, Discord safety)
