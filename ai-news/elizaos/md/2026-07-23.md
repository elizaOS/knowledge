## Crypto Market Conditions: Bear vs Bull Debate and Alt Season Discussion

### Market Structure Views

- Users debated current market classification, with one position holding that a true bull market requires the top 100 coins by market cap to each reach at least 1 billion dollars, referencing late 2020 and mid 2025 as benchmarks
- A contrasting view identified major cap coins like Ethereum as being in a bear market
- A third perspective argued that crypto now moves in sectors rather than as a unified market, given its growth in scale

### Sector-Based Market Activity

- Onchain markets, specifically the Robinhood chain, were identified as currently experiencing a bull market while major coins lag
- Past narratives identified as having driven sector-specific cycles include AI coins, meme coins following the 2024 presidential election, RWA, and the Robinhood chain
- The pattern of attention-driven sector rotation was compared to how attention drove the semiconductor stock market

### Ethereum and Macro Context

- Ethereum broke above its 2021 high in August 2025 after more than five years without a meaningful bull run
- Tightening monetary policy and a poor macro environment over the past five years were cited as contributing factors

### Alt Season Discussion

- One participant argued that a broad alt season similar to 2021 is unlikely to return
- Another participant maintained that alt season is already occurring within specific sectors
- Rising memory and smartphone prices were noted as reflections of broader macro pressures, with cheap memory prices humorously suggested as a potential signal for a broad alt season
