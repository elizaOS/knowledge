## ElizaOS Token Controversy and Community Discussion

### CoinDesk Interview with Shaw Walters

- Shaw Walters, founder of Eliza Labs, gave a CoinDesk interview acknowledging the original AI16Z token was a memecoin
- The migration to ElizaOS was described as an attempt to move beyond memecoin status and carry the project vision forward
- Shaw disclosed he sold his entire ElizaOS token holdings, which had previously been valued at approximately 25 million dollars but had fallen to around 40,000 dollars at the time of sale, citing tax obligations

### Community Response

- Community members expressed skepticism and frustration, with several users labeling the project a scam
- Accusations were raised about insiders continuing to sell tokens at depressed prices
- Bybit was reported to be threatening delisting of the ElizaOS token, with the exchange already applying an ST tag indicating low liquidity
- Some users noted ElizaOS resembles a memecoin but lacks the strong community typically associated with one
- Discussion emerged around Shaw potentially pivoting toward MetaDAO, with some interpreting this as ElizaOS continuing only as an open framework rather than a token-backed project

### Shaw Walters at Stanford Summit 2026

- Shaw Walters participated in a panel titled "AI In Open Systems: Native Participation, Verifiability, and the Future of Autonomous Finance" at the Future of Digital Currency Initiative Stanford Summit 2026
- Panelists included Christian Catalini from the MIT Cryptoeconomics Lab and Professor Ruizhe Jia from Stanford University
- A portion of community members indicated they continued to follow Shaw's work and expressed confidence that development was ongoing
