## ElizaOS Token Collapse and Community Fallout

### Background

- On August 6, 2026, the ElizaOS Discord community engaged in extensive discussion following the apparent abandonment of the ElizaOS token by lead developer Shaw
- Community members clarified that Shaw's own public statement indicated the token was dead while the underlying project remained separate
- The collapse was attributed to a lawsuit filed by law firm Burwick on behalf of token holders

### Sequence of Events

- Burwick filed a lawsuit against ElizaLabs on behalf of disgruntled token holders
- Shaw chose to settle the lawsuit using the project's remaining capital rather than contest it in court
- The settlement drained available funds and led to the token's total collapse
- Shaw publicly announced he would no longer support the token following the settlement

### Community Response

- Long-term holders expressed significant anger, having held through a prolonged bear market based on promises of token buybacks and product releases
- Members noted that major exchanges were still listing ElizaOS at the time of the collapse
- Some holders directed frustration at the minority of litigious holders, with at least one member proposing a counter-lawsuit against the original claimants
- Shaw's departure at what members described as the rock bottom of the bear market was characterized as a sharp betrayal

### Developer Perspective

- One community member identifying as a developer credited Shaw with building a strong open source community and codebase
- This member acknowledged the economic failure while arguing Shaw faced significant external pressures
- The member stated that Eliza broadly accelerated agent development across the space

### Background Claims and Investigations

- Community members referenced Shaw's prior project Webaverse
- Alleged connections through a company called Tin Drum to figures including Marina Abramovic and Tulsi Gabbard were raised
- Members linked to a Washington Post investigation and a Register article concerning a 4 million dollar crypto theft during an in-person meeting

### Pending Legal Action

- Community member paolin reported that law firm Wolf Popper had been briefed and was preparing paperwork for a new class action lawsuit directed personally against Shaw
- The stated purpose was to hold Shaw personally accountable rather than to recover lost funds
- Burwick was characterized by community members as having a poor track record of winning cases
- Discussion included monitoring of potential exchange delistings and the status of related projects including Degenai
