## ElizaOS Community Discussion: Token Migration, Sentiment, and Developer Activity

### Token Migration Concerns

- A user who began holding AI16Z tokens in August 2025 missed the migration window to ELIZAOS and sought community guidance on available options
- The user indicated a lack of awareness about the migration process during the transition period

### Community Sentiment

- Some members expressed optimism, citing an anticipated cross-platform application for Android, iOS, Mac, and PC
- Lead developer Shaw was praised by supporters as a visionary building real AI agentic infrastructure
- Other members noted that Shaw had left the server and was reportedly stepping away from crypto and web3, with intentions to detach his entity from the token while retaining brand ownership

### Critical Assessments of Project Trajectory

- Shaw holds approximately 1 percent of the token supply
- The liquidity pool was described as thin
- Shaw is seeking to sell 5 percent of his company to raise funds for hiring and development
- The structural token allocation was identified as a strategic misstep, with the view that retaining a larger supply for business development would have been preferable
- Multiple community members concluded the token is effectively dead while acknowledging the underlying project could continue independently of token holders
