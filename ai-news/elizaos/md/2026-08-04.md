## ElizaOS Community Discussion - August 4, 2026

### Project Direction and Token Announcements

- Shaw (shawmakesmagic) announced Eliza as the fastest, most powerful, and most consumer-friendly open source agent
- Plans confirmed for launches across iPhone, Android, browser, desktop, and as a full operating system on devices such as the LightPhone 3
- Team disclosed they had been working privately to minimize distractions, with app product releases across all devices described as coming soon
- Shaw publicly acknowledged working through serious health issues, including a frozen shoulder, during the development period

### Token Discussion

- Community member satsbased confirmed in Discord that the Eliza token is effectively done, with the team now focused solely on building working products
- Community debate followed the announcement, with members expressing a range of positions on the token's future
- DannyNOR called for an official statement from the team clarifying the token's role so the community could move forward
- paolin argued that abandoning the token after migration would be unfair to holders who experienced significant losses, and noted that successful real-world products would naturally generate interest in both the project and the token
