## ElizaOS Community Discussion: Project Direction, Token Concerns, and Future App Vision

### Lead Developer Statement on Project Status

- Shaw (shawmakesmagic) disclosed personal financial strain, having lived off savings for approximately six months with no tokens or income
- The foundation treasury was depleted and most of the core team departed
- A prior lawsuit with Burwick Law was settled for $400k, with legal costs otherwise projected to reach $2 million
- A separate ongoing lawsuit involves the opposing party facing potential jail time for withholding discovery
- Shaw stated a clear focus on building AI technology for everyday people, with no interest in crypto-native products such as trading bots or launchpads

### New Product Direction Announced

- Shaw announced a consumer app and operating system experience covering notes, calendar, browser, messaging, wallet, social media, and everyday phone functions
- The interface is driven by chat and voice interaction
- Partnerships were confirmed with a private compute company and a phone company, with ElizaOS positioned as the native OS
- Web3 infrastructure remains available as an optional layer, with crypto intended to function seamlessly in the background
- A video preview was shared with the community

### Community App Ideas Submitted

- Health and fitness companion covering workout optimization, macro tracking, grocery budgeting, and supplement monitoring
- Researcher assistant that writes directly into local documents
- Tax optimization agent identifying legal strategies
- Construction assistant generating bills of materials and schematic drawings for DIY home projects
- Custom social media feed curation
- Community members referenced existing AI construction tools including Beam AI and Buildots as context for the space

### Token and Community Discussion

- Shaw confirmed he holds no tokens and has no control over token operations
- Community members identified two distinct groups: long-term builders holding quietly and a vocal minority of price-focused speculators
- Shaw indicated he would consider creating a new server or wiping channels to refocus community activity on technology
- Multiple community members argued against shutting down the server, citing the presence of loyal contributors and builders

### Developer Activity in Coders Channel

- A developer building DeFi agents raised a question about whether impermanent loss and breakeven calculators require reproducible and verifiable calculation traces for audit purposes
- The developer reported completing a deterministic O(1) calculator for constant-product pools and offered to share the methodology
- A blockchain and full-stack developer with over six years of experience posted availability for project work and client engagements
