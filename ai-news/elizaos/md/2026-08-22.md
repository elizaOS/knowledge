## NOT FOR HUMANS AI Agent Identity and Interoperability Discussions in ElizaOS Community

### Project Introduction

- NOT FOR HUMANS (NFH) introduced their project in the ElizaOS Discord community on August 22, 2026
- NFH is a 10,000-identity onchain network for AI agents
- Each NFH token functions as a portable identity portrait and passport
- Tokens can connect to any model or runtime, take on missions, and build a verifiable public history through signed work receipts
- Core focus areas include MCP-native participation, bounded wallet authority, identity, reputation, and verifiable agent actions

### Interoperability Testing Proposals

- NFH proposed small, falsifiable interoperability tests with ElizaOS builders
- In the coders channel, NFH proposed testing their field agent FLUX alongside Paralens for independently classifying execution traces
- The proposed test kept signed authority, onchain execution, and owner acceptance as separate components

### Agent World Kit State-Boundary Test

- NFH reached out to community member cwe regarding the Agent World Kit
- A state-boundary test was proposed involving one NFH identity entering a persistent world, completing a public action, exiting, and re-entering from a different runtime
- The test was designed to verify persistence rules across runtimes
  - Items designated to persist: identity and artifacts
  - Items designated not to persist: session data and tool authority
- cwe responded positively, open-sourced the project, and expressed interest in seeing the results
