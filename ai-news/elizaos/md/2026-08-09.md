## Token Management Uncertainty and Community Discussion

### Token Control Concerns

- Community members expressed concern and confusion over current token management following a falling out with previous holders
- A Coindesk interview was referenced, with users noting that certain holders went rogue and caused harm to genuine holders
- Users including Taco and Zaday expressed interest in a new group taking over token management
- Zaday suggested a new legal entity should step in and work with Shaw to clarify the transfer of token management responsibilities
- Some users exchanged casual greetings and reconnected after time away
- One user expressed pessimism about the token's future value

### Platform Availability Inquiry
